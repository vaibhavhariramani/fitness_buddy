Features

Everything you track, in one place

Stop juggling a workout logger, a calorie counter, a habit app and a spreadsheet. Fitness Buddy does it all — and it all talks to each other.

Workout logging

Log every set with a smart rest timer, last-time performance, RIR tracking and automatic personal-record detection.

Exercise library

70 exercises with photos, step-by-step instructions, form tips, muscle diagrams and easy substitutions.

Weekly planner

Build routines, schedule your week and start a guided session from any plan — moving a day around is one tap.

Nutrition tracking

Search Open Food Facts, scan barcodes or use Quick Add. Attach photos to meals and hit daily calorie and macro targets.

Body progress

Weight trend, goal progress and personalised BMI, maintenance calories and macro targets — calculated for you.

Wellness reminders

Never forget medicine, yoga or meditation. Pick repeat days and a reminder sound that suits you.

Friends & stories

Add friends, chat, and share 24-hour stories or a workout card to your favourite apps — on your terms.

Expenses & meal ideas

Track spending next to your fitness, get a diet plan from your goals, and see what you can “Cook This” from what you have.

How it works

From sign-up to streak in five steps

A simple daily loop that fits around your life — no setup marathon, no learning curve.

Privacy

Your data stays yours

Health data is personal. Fitness Buddy is built so you're always in control of it.

You choose what friends see

Weight, workouts, streaks, meals and stories each have their own switch. Friends only ever see what you turn on.

Delete anytime

One tap in Settings deletes your account and all your data immediately — no waiting period, no email required.

No ads. No tracking.

No ad networks and no analytics SDKs. It's free, with no paywalls — and you can read every line of the source.

Free and open source

Fitness Buddy is MIT-licensed. Read the code, fork it, and run your own copy on your own Firebase project in about 15 minutes — the setup guide walks through every step.
