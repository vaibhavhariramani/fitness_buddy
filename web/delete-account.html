Delete Your Account & Data

Fitness Buddy · Last updated: 4 September 2026

You can permanently delete your Fitness Buddy account and all data associated with it at any time, directly in the app — no need to contact us.

How to delete your account (in-app)
  1. Open Fitness Buddy and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all of your data are deleted immediately — no waiting period.

Can't get into the app? Email vhariramani01@gmail.com from the email you signed up with and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (sign-in credentials, name, email address)
  • All body-weight, height, and profile data
  • All workout, exercise, and personal-record history
  • All nutrition/food diary entries
  • All expense-tracking entries
  • Progress and story photos you uploaded
  • Your friends list, and chat messages you sent or received
  • Wellness reminders and notification settings

Partial deletion

If you'd like specific data deleted instead of your whole account (for example, only your photos, or only your expense history), say so in your email and we'll delete just that data while keeping your account active.
