Privacy Policy

Fitness Buddy · Last updated: 2 September 2026

Fitness Buddy ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal fitness, nutrition, and workout tracking app. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how you can control or delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, via Google Sign-In or email/password sign-up.
  • Health & fitness data — body weight, height, workout logs, exercise sets/reps/weights, personal records, and food/nutrition diary entries you log yourself.
  • Photos — workout progress photos and social "story" photos you choose to upload.
  • Social data — your friends list and the activity you share with friends inside the app (e.g. workout completions), if you use the social features.
  • Camera — used only to scan food barcodes and to capture photos you choose to add; the app does not access the camera in the background.
  • Device notification token — used to deliver reminders and friend-activity notifications you've enabled; not used for advertising.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, or browsing history, and we do not use the app to serve ads.

How we use this information

  • To provide the app's core features — tracking your workouts, nutrition, and body-weight progress over time.
  • To power features you opt into, such as reminders and sharing activity with friends.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, and Firebase Cloud Storage for photos), Google's cloud infrastructure. Food-search results may also be fetched from the public Open Food Facts database when you search for a packaged food item.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete any workout, nutrition, or weight entry at any time from within the app.
  • You can turn off notifications in the app's Settings.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. See our account deletion page for details on what's removed.

Children's privacy

Fitness Buddy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
