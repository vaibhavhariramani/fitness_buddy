Need help with Fitness Buddy? Here's how to get it.

Contact us

Email vhariramani01@gmail.com — include your device, app version (Settings screen), and what happened. We aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

I can't sign in

  • Check you're using the same sign-in method you signed up with (email/password, Google, or Sign in with Apple).
  • Use Forgot password? on the login screen to reset an email/password account.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Your account and all of your data are deleted immediately. See Delete your account & data for details.

How do I control what friends can see?

Settings → Privacy — what friends can see lets you turn sharing of your weight, workouts, streak, meals, and stories on or off individually.

My reminders aren't going off

  • Make sure notifications are allowed for Fitness Buddy in your device's Settings.
  • Check the reminder is switched on under Wellness → your reminder.

A food is missing from the database

Use Quick Add to enter calories and macros manually, or create a custom food. You can also email us the name of the food and we'll consider adding it.
